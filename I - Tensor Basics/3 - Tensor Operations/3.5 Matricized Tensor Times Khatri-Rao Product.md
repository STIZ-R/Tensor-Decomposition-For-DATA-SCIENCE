# 3.5 Matricized Tensor Times Khatri-Rao Product

The Matricized Tensor Times Khatri-Rao Product (MTTKRP) is used for the CP Decomposition.

## 3.5.1 MTTKRP for 3-way Tensors

Let take $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n \times p}$ and $\mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times r} \text{ and } \mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}.$ It's important that **row dimensions that each match a tensor dimension** and **column dimensions
 math each other**. Then the **MTTKRP** is given by: <br>
 $\mathbf{U} = \mathbf{X_{(1)}}(\mathbf{C} \odot \mathbf{B}) \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r},$

> **Definition (TTM in Mode 3)** <br>
> Let $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n \times p}, \mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}, \mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times r}, \text{ and } \mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times r}.$ The 3-way **MTTKRPs** are defined as follows: <br>
> Mode-1 MTTKRP: $\mathbf{U} = \mathbf{X_{(1)}}(\mathbf{C} \odot \mathbf{B}) \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r},$ <br>
> Mode-2 MTTKRP: $\mathbf{V} = \mathbf{X_{(2)}}(\mathbf{C} \odot \mathbf{A}) \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times r},$ <br>
> Mode-3 MTTKRP: $\mathbf{W} = \mathbf{X_{(3)}}(\mathbf{B} \odot \mathbf{A}) \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times r}.$
