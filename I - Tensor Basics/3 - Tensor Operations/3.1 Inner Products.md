# 3.1 Inner Products

The tensor product is the higher-order generalization of the dot product.

## 3.1.1 Inner Products fro 3-way Tensors

The **inner product** for 2 3-way tensors of the **same size** is the sum of the products of the corresponding entries and it give a scalar as a result.

> **Definition (Inner Product 3-way)** <br>
> The **Inner product** of tensors $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$, both of size $m \times n \times p$, is <br>
> $\langle \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y} \rangle = \sum_{i=1}^m\sum_{j=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^p x_{ijk}y_{ijk}$.

> **Definition (Tensor Norm 3-way)** <br>
> The **norm** of tensor $\mathbf{x}$ of size $m \times n \times p$ is <br>
> $\lVert\mathbf{x}\rVert = \sqrt{\langle\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}\rangle} = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^m\sum_{j=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^px_{ijk}^2}.$

<br>

For 3-way tensors of size $m \times n \times p$, the computational complexity of both the inner product and the norm is $\mathcal{O}(mnp)$.

<br>

Let take the following 3-way tensors $\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}$:
```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{x}(:,:,1) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{x}(:,:,2) = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 6 \\ 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
\mathbf{y}(:,:,1) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{y}(:,:,2) = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
``` 
We compute the inner product as $(1\cdot 1) + (2\cdot 0) + (3\cdot 0) + (4\cdot 1) + (0\cdot 2) + (6\cdot 1) + (8\cdot 1) + (9\cdot 0) = 19$
<br>
And the norm of the tensor $\mathbf{x}$ is: $\sqrt{1²+2²+3²+4²+0²+6²+8²+9²} = \sqrt{211}$

<br>

## 3.1.2 Inner Products for *d*-way Tensors

Like before, tensors must be the same size to compute the inner product.

> **Definition (Inner Product *d*_way)** <br>
> The **inner product** of tensors $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$, both of size $n_1 \times n_2 \times \dots \times n_d$ is <br>
> $\langle\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\rangle = \sum_{i_1 = 1}^{n_1}\sum_{i_2 = 1}^{n_2}\dots\sum_{i_d = 1}^{n_d} x_{i_1i_2\dots i_d}y_{i_1i_2\dots i_d}.$

<br>

> **Definition (Tensor Norm *d*-way)** <br>
> The **norm** of tensor $\mathbf{x}$ of size $n_1 \times n_2 \times n_d$ is <br>
> $\lVert\mathbf{x}\rVert = \sqrt{\langle\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}\rangle} = \sqrt{\sum_{i_1=1}^{n_1}\sum_{i_2=1}^{n_2}\dots\sum_{i_d=1}^{n_d}x_{i_1i_2\dots i_d}^2}.$

<br>

For a *d*-way tensors of size $n_1 \times n_2 \times \dots \times n_d$,the computational complexity of both the inner product and the norm is $\mathcal{O}(N)$, where $N$ is the total number of entries.
