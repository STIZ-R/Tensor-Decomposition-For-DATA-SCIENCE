# 3.3 Tensor-Times-Matrix Products

> **Definition (Tensor-Times-Matrix Product)** <br>
> The mode-*k* **TTM product** of a tensor $\mathbf{x}$ with a matrix $\mathbf{U}$ is denoted as $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{x} \times_k \mathbf{U}$ and defined in terms of the matricized expression: <br>
> $\mathbf{Y_{(k)}} = \mathbf{UX_{(k)}}$.

So in other words, the TTM $\mathbf{x} \times_k \mathbf{U}$ multiplies each mode-*k* fiber of $\mathbf{x}$ by $\mathbf{U}$.

<br>

## 3.3.1 TTM for 3-way Tensors

> **Definition (TTM in Mode 1)** <br>
> The **TTM operation in mode 1** of $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n \times p}$ with a matrix $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbb{R}^{q \times m}$ is denoted as <br>
> $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{x} \times_1 \mathbf{U}$ <br>
> and produces a $q \times n \times p$ tensor where he column (mode-1) fibers of $\mathbf{y}$ are equal to the corresponding column fibers of $\mathbf{x}$ multiplied by $\mathbf{U}$. Specially, $\mathbf{Y_{(1)}} = \mathbf{UX_{(1)}}$ or <br>
> $\mathbf{Y_{\alpha jk}} = \sum_{i=1}^mx_{ijk}u_{\alpha i}$ for all $(\alpha, j, k) \in [q] \otimes [n] \otimes [p].$
