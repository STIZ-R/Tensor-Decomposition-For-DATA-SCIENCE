# 3.3 Tensor-Times-Matrix Products

> **Definition (Tensor-Times-Matrix Product)** <br>
> The mode-*k* **TTM product** of a tensor $\mathbf{x}$ with a matrix $\mathbf{U}$ is denoted as $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{x} \times_k \mathbf{U}$ and defined in terms of the matricized expression: <br>
> $\mathbf{Y_{(k)} = \mathbf{UX_{(k)}}$.
