# 3.4 TTM in Multiple Modes

When we compute the TTMs, the order of the multiplications does not matter.
Indeed,
> **Proposition (TTM Order)** <br>
> *Let* $\mathbf{x}$ *be a tensor such that mode* $k$ *is of size* $m$ *and mode* $l$ *is of size* $n$*. *Then, for any* $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbb{R}^{q \times m}$ *and* $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times n}$ *with* $k \neq l$, *whe have,* <br>
> $( \mathbf{x} \times_k \mathbf{U}) \times_l \mathbf{V} = (\mathbf{x} \times_l \mathbf{V}) \times_k \mathbf{U}.$
